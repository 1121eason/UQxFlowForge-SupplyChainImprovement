## A concise introduction

In a university team project, I participated in analysing and redesigning a component repair, inventory and logistics process. My contribution covered process analysis, AS-IS and TO-BE modelling, improvement design, quantitative analysis and report preparation.

The business question was how to support more customers while retaining repair quality, tracking and service commitments. We identified repeated record maintenance, repair escalation and weak part/status visibility as the top-three issues. We linked seven changes to those issues, preserved necessary controls and tested the assumptions behind the estimated benefits.

One useful finding was that automation can add operational burden: under our stated inventory scenario, additional scanning reached break-even at about 13.15 seconds per scan. That supported a selective pilot with measurable acceptance criteria. These were academic proposals rather than implemented savings.

## 繁體中文說明重點

我在團隊專案中參與流程分析、AS-IS／TO-BE 建模、改善方案、量化分析與報告撰寫。我的說明重點是把流程問題、根因、改善機制與驗收指標連起來，並檢查改善方案會新增哪些工作。

例如，庫存掃描能改善可追溯性，但也增加現場操作時間。我們把新增負擔納入敏感度分析，而不是把整段盤點時間都算成可節省工時。維修方面則保留最終測試，只讓證據足夠的案件採取精準處理。

面試時可以展示流程圖與計算邊界，說明自己如何做取捨。AU$666 是特定族群及假設下的產能價值估計，不是已替企業節省的現金。
