# Attribution and usage

This repository documents a University of Queensland BISM7216 team project, October 2026, applying process modelling, Lean analysis and quantitative scenarios to repair operations, inventory control and reverse logistics. The fictional FlowForge Solutions case originates in course teaching material.

Yi Cheng Lee (Eason) contributed to process analysis, AS-IS/TO-BE modelling, improvement design, quantitative analysis and report preparation as part of the project team.

The full assignment brief, rubric, original report and presentation script are not redistributed here. Public documentation summarises the analysis and attributes the academic context. The two supplied BPMN artifacts are retained without byte changes under descriptive filenames. Earlier working packages are excluded to avoid mixing model versions.

No blanket open-source licence is applied to the mixed team/course-derived material. Public visibility does not grant rights to reuse university material or other contributors' work. All relevant rights remain with their respective holders.

SAP Signavio and other product names identify tools or case participants. No university or vendor endorsement is implied. AI assisted project documentation, preview generation and reproduction tooling; [provenance](docs/evidence/README.md) distinguishes the source analysis from supporting project artifacts.
